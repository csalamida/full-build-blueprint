# Walk through the build and find small missing things

Read for a full blueprint or missing-pieces audit. Select areas in scope. Ask: “If I tried to build this now, what would I stop and ask for?” Then: “If staff used it tomorrow, what could be unclear or go wrong?”

## Access and ownership

- Account owner, access grantor, required plan/features and permissions.
- System operator, decision approver and cover during absence.
- n8n/integration hosting, costs, connections, backups, error alerts and expired access.
- Keep secrets out of documents and HTML. Explain how authorised staff provides them securely.

## Records, fields, forms and stages

- Separate person, business, job and enquiry records; explicit links between them.
- Several active enquiries for one person without overwritten signing, payment or follow-up progress.
- Automatic versus staff stage changes; proof that each business event occurred.
- Duplicate contacts, changed details, missing data, invalid form submissions and multiple lead sources.
- Form labels/options, required fields, validation, confirmation copy, routing and alerts.
- Views for today's work, overdue work and waiting items.

## Calendars

- Owner, timezone, call length, slot spacing, hours, holidays, buffers, minimum notice and booking window.
- Busy-calendar source, access and staff cover.
- Who calls whom, meeting location/link, booking answers and assignment.
- Confirmation/reminder copy, cancellation, rescheduling, no-show and completed-call actions.
- One sender per message. Rescheduling cancels old reminders; past reminder times are handled deliberately.

## Emails, copy and appearance

- Approved From name/address, monitored Reply-To, signature and reply owner.
- Sending setup, delivery tests and reply routing in the actual account.
- Actual subjects/body, spacing, supplied brand/logo rules, mobile display and plain-text fallback where relevant.
- Exact recipient and record-specific details; missing names, invalid addresses and broken links/files.
- Timing/timezone, count, first personal follow-up, quiet hours where relevant, pauses and stop rules.
- Appropriate Do Not Disturb/opt-out handling for the message type.
- Decide whether CRM, calendar, integration or staff sends each message; avoid two senders.
- Do not silently add SMS or marketing campaigns outside scope.

## Files, signing and payments

- Approved template/version, content owner, filled-in details and sample output.
- Correct signers/order and exact job/enquiry; old or replaced documents cannot release current actions.
- Invoice/payment reference, amount/balance checks and manual transfers. Signing-first and payment-first paths reach the same agreed result.
- Folder layout, naming, reuse, file versions and staff/customer permissions.
- Customer links expose only the intended approved file, not private folders.
- Missing files hold delivery and create useful staff work. Recovery does not repeat completed steps.

## Intake and connections

- Real route: original email, form, supported API or agreed source. Do not assume marketplace APIs or automatic publishing.
- Original samples, stable record references, optional/missing fields and staff review of unclear matches.
- Repeat-message rule versus a genuine new enquiry.
- Saved results sufficient to know what happened and resume safely.
- On an uncertain send result, check provider acceptance before retrying. Bound retries and alert a person when unresolved.
- Simultaneous updates, late events, changed prices/versions and closed or under-offer records where relevant.

## Data move and launch

- Records, notes, history, activities, attachments, relationships, exclusions and sample review.
- Source/destination counts, original dates, follow-up dates and access to older information.
- Suppress unintended messages during imports.
- Which system staff updates during changeover, handling late changes and recovery if launch fails.
- Full-journey tests, approval, staff instructions, account-access handover and agreed support period.
- Daily error checks and the responsible person.

## Finish the walkthrough

Trace a normal journey from source to final business result. Then a second job for the same person, missing file, unclear match, repeat event, reply, cancelled/rescheduled appointment and failed action where applicable. Inspect handoffs: the next stage receives the right information and does not assume staff work happened just because a task was created.

Repair instructions in their stage or shared setup. Combine unanswered client questions upfront, showing why each is needed and which work it affects. Keep later reviews of built work separate. Identify what only actual account tests can prove. Do not transplant another client's thresholds, legal wording, fees, timelines or software choices.
